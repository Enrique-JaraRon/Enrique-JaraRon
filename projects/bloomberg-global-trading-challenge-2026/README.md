# Bloomberg Global Trading Challenge 2026

**ESILV Team · Captain · October–November 2026**

## Project overview

I am participating as team captain in the 2026 Bloomberg Global Trading Challenge, a global, team-based simulated equity investing competition.

The project brings together **quantitative equity research, portfolio risk management, backtesting and collaboration**. Our team's work focuses on making research-driven investment decisions and comparing portfolio performance with the competition benchmark.

## Areas of work

- **Quantitative research:** investigating cross-sectional stock signals and evaluating model stability.
- **Portfolio risk:** understanding covariance, diversification and exposure constraints.
- **Testing:** considering out-of-sample performance, estimation risk and transaction costs.
- **Engineering:** using Python for data analysis and coordinating contributions through Git.
- **Team leadership:** organising research, reviewing evidence and communicating portfolio decisions.

## Public availability

The competition is ongoing. The **collaborative codebase and operational details are private**, so this page presents my role and the project's research themes only.

This is not a public trading algorithm, a reproducible backtest or a claim of realised investment performance. Results will be discussed only once verified and suitable for publication.

*Simulated educational competition. Not investment advice.*
